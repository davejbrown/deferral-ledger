The map is a schematic: major corridors follow their real routes approximately, and local streets are generated around each town. No condition, traffic or cost figure here describes a real road. In North Carolina the state maintains roads outside city limits and counties maintain none, so a real version would be run for the cities or for the state transportation divisions that cover these counties.

Unit costs run from about $3.50 per square yard for a surface seal at PCI 85 to about $118 for full reconstruction near PCI 12, interpolated between. Roads lose 1 to 5 PCI points a year, fastest between 50 and 70 and on busier streets. A treated road returns to PCI 93 (seal) or 97 (everything heavier). Roads at PCI 85 and above need nothing. Failed means below PCI 35. Costs are held in today's dollars with no inflation.
